## What's Happening

Recent review of the HSE Safety Dashboard reveals that **Depot B has recorded 33 slip and trip incidents** -- more than any other facility in our network and nearly double the next highest site.

Alarmingly, **82% of these incidents occurred during the Night Shift**, concentrated around the transfer walkway. Ten of these occurrences were classified as high-potential serious injury events (Potential SIFs).

Nobody has suffered a life-altering disability yet, and our goal is to keep it that way.

---

## Why This Matters to You

If you work night operations at Depot B, you walk through this corridor every single shift under varying lighting and ambient moisture conditions.

**This is not about blame or policing how you walk** -- it is about identifying an environmental hazard that is catching team members off guard. We would rather listen, fix the physical root causes, and eliminate the risk today than conduct a serious injury investigation tomorrow.

---

## What We Need You to Do -- Starting Today

- **Slow down through the transfer walkway**, particularly during the first and last hours of the shift when fatigue peaks.
- **Wear your company-issued slip-resistant footwear** on every shift -- no personal trainers or worn-out treads.
- **Report wet, oily, or poorly lit areas immediately** to your supervisor so facilities can deploy absorbent mats and lighting crews.
- **Keep three points of contact on stairs and handrails** near the walkway ramps.
- **Speak up in your pre-shift huddle:** If you had a near-slip or spotted moisture pooling, raise it immediately without fear of reprimand.
